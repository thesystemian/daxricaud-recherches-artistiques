RESEARCH 02 VERSION 0.1

Les Convocations du milieu

How can a local maritime signal summon an action, then produce a trace and an archive?

State
Emerging
Media
Protocol, action, data, archive
First direction
Signaux de rive
Open the research

A milieu produces a signal. The signal summons an action. This response may be completed, delayed, prevented, refused or remain impossible to verify. The research observes the gap between the signal, the action and the archive that attempts to retain its trace.

RESEARCH 01 VERSION 0.1

Temporal Signatures

How do a date and time become a form, an identifier and a point of closure?

State
Active
Media
Drawing, notation, protocol
Latest closure
04.10.26 | 14:18
Open the research

For several years, Dax Ricaud has inscribed the completion date and time on his drawings. The notation changes through bubbles, frames, lines, pipes and handwritten marks. This research observes the moment when information becomes a visual element.

METHOD

Each research project has its own temporality.

A work can be completed. Research may remain open, become interrupted, resume or give rise to another form.

States do not rank success. The protocol makes visible the conditions, variations, passages and moments of closure.
