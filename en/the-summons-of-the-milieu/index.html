RESEARCH 02 / EMERGING

The Summons of the Milieu

How can a local maritime signal summon an action, then produce an inscription and an archive?

State

WORKING PRINCIPLE

A milieu produces a signal. The signal summons an action.

Hypothesis

The principle and chain below describe a proposed protocol, not a completed action or a demonstrated signal-response relationship.

The research organises an encounter between a remotely transmitted flow, a situated body and a support capable of retaining an inscription.

Data does not mechanically command the body. It summons it. The response may be completed, delayed, prevented, refused or remain impossible to verify.

The work lies neither in the data nor in the action alone. It is built in the gap between them, in the conditions of the response and in the necessarily incomplete archive that attempts to retain it.

INDEXICAL CHAIN

From flow to register.

Each passage may introduce loss, delay, contradiction or prevention. These gaps are recorded rather than erased afterwards.

  1. 01Milieu

    A maritime, meteorological or port context.

  2. 02Flow

    Data received through an identifiable source.

  3. 03Signal

    An element of the flow is selected by a rule.

  4. 04Summons

    The signal opens the possibility of a response.

  5. 05Action

    The body completes, delays, refuses or cannot act.

  6. 06Inscription

    A material, optical, temporal or textual form remains.

  7. 07Register

    Data, responses, gaps and documents are connected.

02.01

FIRST DIRECTION

Shore Signals

Unknown

No proven signal or completed occurrence is documented to date. The source, selection rule and reception conditions remain to be established and verified.

Around Port Louis, a maritime or port signal could summon a situated action, then produce an inscription and a documentary deposit.

Signal

Real, dated data from an identifiable source, including its delays and limits.

Response

A run, a walk, deliberate stillness, a delay or an explicitly stated refusal.

Inscription

A graphic notation, photograph, short video or dated text.

Archive

A lightweight register that also retains absences, contradictions and prevented actions.
