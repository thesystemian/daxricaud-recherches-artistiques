RESEARCH 03 / EMERGING

VERSION 0.1

Studies of the Ordinary

How can an ordinary action, actually carried out, produce a form without being embellished, dramatised or explained in advance?

INTRODUCTION

The body acts. The camera observes. Thought intervenes afterwards.

Performative, video-based, poetic and digital research

Studies of the Ordinary is a daily artistic practice, with no required frequency or outcome, in which lived actions become filmed performances and subsequently give rise to poems. Each ensemble is composed and presented in HTML. The research develops at the pace of the artist’s life.

After the action, an account of the experience and a private conversation with artificial intelligence allow the artist to choose a question. He responds through a personal Poème de l’après-coup, or after-the-event poem, whose form remains free.

The recording, question and poem may then be organised within an HTML form. Each study remains autonomous while joining a versioned archive that may reveal transformations over several years.

CHAIN OF THE STUDY

From everyday gesture to HTML form.

The method may remain stable without imposing a schedule, a depth or an identical form on every study.

  1. 01Action

    An ordinary action, preferably one that is genuinely necessary.

  2. 02Camera

    A fixed shot or a recording device suited to the situation.

  3. 03Performance

    The gesture is carried out without requiring a spectacular event.

  4. 04Account

    The artist reports the lived experience after the action.

  5. 05Conversation

    A private exchange examines facts, sensations and gaps.

  6. 06Question

    Dax chooses the question to which he wishes to respond.

  7. 07Poem

    A Poème de l’après-coup is written by the artist.

  8. 08HTML and archive

    The selected elements are organised and their versions preserved.

03.01

FIRST CASES

Three studies documented in the private archive.

Documented fact

The three actions, their recordings and their Poèmes de l’après-coup exist. The recordings, poems and conversations remain private. The HTML forms are not published, and some have yet to be built.

Sweeping

A real household task becomes the starting point for a recording, a question and a poem.

In Front of the Fan

The body enters domestic heat and the airflow produced by an ordinary machine.

Standing Facing the Camera with Hands on Hips

A posture held before the device opens another relationship between observation, duration and reflection on experience.

Evolving territory

The research begins at home and may move outdoors towards walking, waiting, carrying or picking things up.

ARCHIVE AND VERSIONS

The conversational sketches remain private.

03

Conversations before and after the performance belong to each study as studio sketches. Originals and successive versions of recordings, poems, protocols and HTML files are preserved without being automatically entered into the public Register.

OPEN STATE

A study can be a work without ceasing to be research.

Here, the word “study” does not indicate an inferior draft. It preserves the possibility of testing, comparing and transforming. The artistic status of each realisation must nevertheless be tested through its form, necessity and presentation.
