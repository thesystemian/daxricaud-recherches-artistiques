The Summons of the Milieu

How can a local maritime signal summon an action, then produce a trace and an archive?

METHOD

Each research project has its own temporality.

A work can be completed. Research may remain open, become interrupted, resume or give rise to another form.

States do not indicate degrees of success. The protocol makes visible the conditions, variations, passages and moments of closure.

CRITICAL LEGEND

Distinguish what is documented from what remains to be tested.

Documented fact
An element supported by a document or an identified source, within the limits of that evidence.
Interpretation
A reading proposed from the documents, distinct from what they directly establish.
Hypothesis
A proposition or protocol to be tested.
Unverified
Information mentioned whose verification has not been established.
Unknown
Missing information or a question unanswered at this stage.
